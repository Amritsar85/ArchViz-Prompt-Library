# MATERIAL — Concept Directions

Explore contrasting but architecturally coherent material strategies, appropriate to the site and intended use. Consider tactile character, scale, light response, joints, weathering and relationship to neighboring buildings.

Preserve the selected form and spatial design. Change the material palette and its architectural expression, not the underlying project geometry.

Avoid implausible material spans, invented details that conflict with design constraints and generic CGI sheen.
