# VARIATIONS — Three Distinct Directions

Generate three clearly differentiated conceptual directions for the project: A, B and C.

A — Contextual and restrained: integrate with local scale and materials.
B — Contemporary and expressive: explore articulation, rhythm and strong spatial identity.
C — Landscape-led and porous: prioritize outdoor space, transitions, shade and permeability.

Keep fixed constraints consistent. If the tool can generate only one image at a time, generate each direction separately with the same reference view and camera.
