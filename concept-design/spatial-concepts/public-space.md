# SPACE — Public Realm Concept

Explore spatial ideas for how the existing public space could be used and experienced. Study circulation, gathering areas, shade, thresholds, places to sit, planting, visibility and transitions between buildings and open space.

Keep the defined site boundaries and fixed access points. Introduce only ideas plausible for the given context, and show a readable relationship between activities and physical layout.

This is an exploratory visual study; accessibility, safety and technical requirements need separate review.
