# CONCEPT_MASTER — Architectural Concept Exploration

Explore original architectural design ideas for the existing project or site. Use the provided context, program and massing as a starting framework rather than as a finished design.

Develop coherent architectural directions considering massing, relationships between solids and voids, rhythm, access, human scale, daylight, circulation and relationship to the surroundings.

Propose distinct, recognizable design approaches instead of superficial color or material changes. Respect any explicitly defined site boundaries, fixed structures and functional requirements.

Favor clear architectural intentions, believable building logic and compelling spatial qualities over arbitrary formal complexity.

Treat the output as an early design study, not a construction-ready or code-compliant design. Avoid presenting unverified technical assumptions as facts.
