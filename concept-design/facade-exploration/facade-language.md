# FACADE — Architectural Language

Explore alternative facade expressions for the existing building volumes. Investigate proportion, solid-to-void ratio, shading devices, balcony rhythm, material layering, recesses and depth of facade articulation.

Preserve the overall building massing and camera viewpoint unless explicitly authorized otherwise. Keep openings and structural strategies visually plausible; do not imply technical validation.

Produce coherent architectural concepts rather than decorative textures applied arbitrarily.
