# MASSING — Volume Variations

Develop three distinct architectural massing concepts from the current volume study. Explore controlled operations such as setbacks, stepped forms, courtyards, terraces, articulated rooflines, voids and changes in building rhythm.

Keep the site context and any fixed footprint, height or circulation constraints explicitly supplied. Show coherent scale relationships and plausible structural continuity.

Make the alternatives genuinely different in spatial strategy, not merely material finish. Do not invent additional site area.
