# Facial fidelity

Preserve each existing person's facial identity, apparent age, expression, gaze, head angle, skin tone, hair and visible facial features. Keep the original profile, occlusion and level of detail at the person's scale. For distant, partially hidden or indistinct faces, retain only what the source resolves. Do not invent features, replace faces, beautify skin or turn heads toward the camera. Limit photographic adjustments to lighting, colour balance and grain consistent with the scene.
