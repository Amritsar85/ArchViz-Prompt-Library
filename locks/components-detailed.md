# LOCK - Detailed facade components

> Texte de T10/T11. Les volets repliés restent parfois modifiés.

Preserve the exact design of all shutters, window frames, door frames and opening surrounds shown in the source image.

Maintain their original dimensions, profiles, divisions, thicknesses, colors and positions. Preserve the number, arrangement and opening angles of shutter panels, including their slat direction.

Preserve roof edges, copings, flashings and eaves exactly as shown. Do not add exposed tiles where the source shows a smooth edge or coping.

Improve only their photographic material appearance and lighting, without redesigning or replacing these components.
