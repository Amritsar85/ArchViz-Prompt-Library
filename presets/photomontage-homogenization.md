# Homogénéisation de photomontage

Edit this existing architectural photomontage.

HOMOGENIZATION ONLY. Preserve the exact geometry, architecture, heights, proportions, perspective, framing, street slope, sidewalks, road, walls, poles, cables and all object positions. Do not redesign, move, add or remove anything.

Unify the photographic rendering so the inserted building blends naturally with the original street photograph. Match the same daylight atmosphere, sun direction, exposure, colour balance, contrast, lens softness, shadow softness, atmospheric depth and material realism. Reduce the clean CGI appearance and improve contact shadows, facade junctions and ground integration. Keep the project clean and realistic, but avoid perfectly flat synthetic surfaces.

PRESERVE ORIGINAL BUILDING COLOURS. Keep the original colour of every building and material in the source photomontage, including façades, plinths, shutters, window frames, railings and roofs. Preserve the colour differences between buildings, between façade sections and between the new construction and its surroundings. Adjust exposure and shadows to integrate the inserted building while preserving each material’s underlying hue and saturation. Do not recolour, bleach or apply a uniform tint to the buildings. This colour preservation takes priority over any colour balance adjustment.

Keep the inserted building's plaster clean and newly finished. Preserve its subtle mineral grain and gentle natural tonal variation. Avoid dirt, dark streaks, splash marks and weathering on the inserted façades, especially at their bases and around openings. Retain genuine contact shadows and the existing base courses. Leave the patina of surrounding older buildings and natural pavement texture intact.

Make the whole image feel like a single real photograph.

Avoid painterly effects, oversharpening, blur, bloom, orange cast, fake texture overlays, heavy stylization or architectural changes.
