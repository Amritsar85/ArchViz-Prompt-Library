# PRESET — Concept Massing Exploration

Explore three different architectural massing concepts based on the site and provided initial volumes. Treat the current forms as a starting framework, not as an approved final design.

Keep the existing terrain, site boundaries, surroundings, access constraints and camera viewpoint. Investigate three distinct strategies: an articulated courtyard organization, a stepped terrace organization, and a compact composition with carefully positioned voids.

Maintain believable human scale, coherent circulation, sunlight access and plausible structural continuity. Do not invent additional land area or alter immovable existing buildings.

Show clear differences between options while maintaining a consistent visual presentation. These are early-stage concept studies, not verified construction designs.
