# PRESET — Object Integration

> Prompt assemblé, prêt à copier dans Gemini.

Add the requested object naturally within the specified area of the existing scene.

Respect the original camera perspective, object scale, environmental lighting, contact shadows, reflections and spatial relationships.

Ensure that the new object is physically plausible and correctly integrated with surrounding surfaces and existing architecture.

Preserve all unrelated structures, materials, vegetation and environmental elements.

Do not alter the camera framing or redesign the surrounding scene.

Do not redesign, relocate, remove, or invent architectural elements.

Preserve all buildings, rooflines, openings, structural relationships, material identities, and major spatial organization.

Preserve the existing composition, camera position, perspective, framing, and field of view.

Do not crop, zoom, rotate, or significantly recompose the scene.

Preserve the overall site layout, circulation structure, planting arrangement, ground levels and landscape organization.

Do not introduce unrelated landscape features or modify the project layout.
