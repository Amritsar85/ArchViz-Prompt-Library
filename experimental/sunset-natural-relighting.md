# EXPERIMENTAL - Natural sunset relighting (T22)

> Entrée : image T11 déjà générée. T22 trop clair pour la nuit ; briques adoucies, volets et personnages modifiés.

Change only the lighting and atmosphere of this architectural rendering to a subtle golden sunset.

Preserve the existing camera, framing, geometry, architectural details, material assignments, facade color differences and objects.

Introduce low-angle golden sunlight from outside the right edge of the frame. Let it gently illuminate appropriately oriented surfaces and foliage, balanced with cooler blue ambient skylight in shaded areas. Keep the architecture readable and retain natural material colors.

Adapt the sky, shadows, exposure and glazing reflections coherently to the sunset. Avoid excessive orange or pink coloration, lens flare, bloom and dramatic foreground shadow patterns.

Retain the existing richness, scale and irregularity of surface textures, especially grass-filled concrete pavers, individual grass tufts, asphalt aggregate, brick joints and render grain. Do not smooth, regularize, replace or reinterpret these textures. Preserve fine shutter, window-frame and railing details.

Keep all areas clearly resolved, including surrounding buildings and vegetation. Do not introduce artistic blur, selective focus or sharpening halos.

Do not add interior lighting, landscape lighting, illuminated fixtures or new objects. This pass changes the natural lighting and atmosphere only.

Preserve the original aspect ratio without cropping or recomposing.
