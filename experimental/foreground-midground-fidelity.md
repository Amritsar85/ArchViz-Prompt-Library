# EXPERIMENTAL — Foreground and Midground Fidelity

> Bloc expérimental, non validé isolément. Voir tests/2026-10-29-t01-t08.md. Copier uniquement le corps anglais.

Give particular priority to preserving foreground and midground features. Keep every existing boat and person in its original position, with its original overall silhouette, proportions and orientation. Do not add, remove, duplicate or relocate boats or people.

Preserve the exact outlines and boundaries of the foreground quay, harbor walls and tower. Preserve building volumes, rooflines, and the positions, shapes and dimensions of existing openings. Keep solid walls solid; do not invent windows or doors.

Photographic texture enhancement is allowed within these preserved boundaries. Improve surface detail, roughness, reflections and material richness while retaining each material's identity. Do not use texture enhancement to alter geometry or introduce new architectural features.

These priorities do not authorize changes to background architecture. Preserve background building placement and silhouettes as well.
