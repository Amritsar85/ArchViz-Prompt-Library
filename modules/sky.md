# SKY_01 — Natural Sky and Distant Background

Improve the realism of the sky, distant background, and horizon while preserving the overall atmosphere of the scene.

Create a natural sky with believable cloud structure, soft tonal variation, and realistic atmospheric perspective.

Enhance distant background elements so they feel coherent and photographic, without drawing too much attention away from the architecture.

Preserve the established weather conditions and general time of day.

Avoid artificial cloud formations, overdramatic skies, excessive haze, or unrealistic background sharpness.
