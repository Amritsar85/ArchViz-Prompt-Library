# PEOPLE_01 — Realistic Human Figures

Improve the realism of people in the scene while preserving their approximate positions and the overall activity distribution.

Make the figures appear more natural and believable, with realistic human proportions, clothing appropriate to the environment, varied postures, distinct silhouettes, and plausible small-group behavior.

Ensure they are correctly scaled relative to the architecture and integrated with coherent shadows and perspective.

Maintain believable interactions between people and their environment.

Avoid repeated figures, stiff poses, blurry people, unnatural body proportions and inconsistent scale.
