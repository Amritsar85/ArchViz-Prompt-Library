# MATERIALS_03 — Clean Facades / Pristine Architectural Finishes

Keep exterior plaster, render, stucco and painted facades clean, evenly finished and well maintained. Preserve the intended color and overall material uniformity while retaining fine, natural microtexture, realistic roughness and credible lighting.

Do not introduce patchy discoloration, stains, grime, algae, runoff streaks, weathering, peeling or aging. Avoid plastic-looking flatness and repetitive procedural noise. Do not alter facade geometry, windows or architectural details.
