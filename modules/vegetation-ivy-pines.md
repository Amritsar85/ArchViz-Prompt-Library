# Ivy and pine detail

Where ivy or another trailing plant is visible, follow its existing growth from the visible support or planter. Preserve the direction, unequal lengths and gaps of its stems, the apparent leaf size, and the areas of facade and railing visible between them. Retain the visible leaf shapes and colours of each plant. Avoid repeating identical strands or extending foliage across bare surfaces.

Where pines are visible, match each tree’s trunk, branching pattern, crown outline and gaps through which the sky or architecture is visible. Preserve the grouping of needle-bearing shoots and the differences in green between trees and between sunlit and shaded foliage. Resolve needles only where the source image resolves them. Keep distant crowns readable at their original scale.
