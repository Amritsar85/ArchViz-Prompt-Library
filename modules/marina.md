# MARINA_01 — Boats and Marina Realism

Improve the realism of the boats and marina elements while preserving the existing scene composition and harbor layout.

Make the vessels appear more authentic, with believable hull proportions, glazing, rigging, deck details, subtle wear, realistic reflections, and coherent contact with the water.

Where appropriate, refine generic or artificial-looking boats into more convincing yachts, sailboats, or marina vessels suited to the scene, without changing their intended positions or types.

Maintain correct scale, natural positioning, and believable spacing.

Avoid distorted boats, repeated vessel designs, awkward placements, or unrealistic maritime activity.
