# MATERIALS_01 — Architectural Materials

Enhance the realism of architectural surfaces and construction materials while preserving the original design.

Improve stone, plaster, concrete, metal, glazing, paving, and roof materials where present, with more believable texture definition, faithful base colors, realistic roughness, and convincing contact shadows. Preserve the intended cleanliness and finish quality of each material.

Preserve the intended material identity, color palette, and architectural character of the project.

Keep all materials appropriate to the project and environment.

Do not add dirt, stains, discoloration, weathering, aging, water streaks, damage, mold or surface grime by default. Do not confuse realistic texture with material deterioration. Use the optional weathering module only when explicitly requested.
