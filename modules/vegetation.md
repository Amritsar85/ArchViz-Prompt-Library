# VEGETATION_01 — Natural Vegetation

Improve the realism of the vegetation while preserving its existing layout, positions, silhouettes, and landscaping structure.

Make trees, shrubs, and planted areas appear more natural and botanically believable, with distinct foliage masses, irregular canopy shapes, realistic density variation, and natural color differences.

Preserve fine vegetation detail and improve the interaction of light and shadow within planted areas.

Maintain vegetation species and characteristics consistent with the existing environment.

Avoid blurry foliage, painterly vegetation, artificial repetition, or oversmoothed plant textures.
