# LIGHTING_01 — Natural Photographic Lighting

Refine the scene with a natural photographic atmosphere and believable lighting.

Use coherent light direction, realistic shadow behavior, balanced exposure, subtle atmospheric depth, and natural color relationships.

Preserve the existing time of day, general weather conditions and principal lighting direction.

Maintain a professional architectural photography feel rather than a stylized or cinematic look.

Avoid excessive haze, artificial bloom, dramatic grading, unrealistic glow, or exaggerated HDR contrast.
