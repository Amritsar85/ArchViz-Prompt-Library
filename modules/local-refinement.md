# LOCAL_01 — Localized Photorealistic Refinement

Refine only the specified area of the image to improve its photographic realism.

Preserve the existing geometry, perspective, scale and spatial relationships within the edited region.

Match surrounding materials, lighting, shadows, reflections, color balance and photographic sharpness.

Keep all unrelated areas unchanged.

Do not modify the overall camera composition or introduce unrelated objects.

This is a localized refinement task, not a full scene redesign.
