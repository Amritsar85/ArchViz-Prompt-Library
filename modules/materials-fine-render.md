# MODULE - Fine facade render

> Option réservée aux façades enduites. T24 apprécié après T23 ; effet causal non isolé par répétitions.

Keep rendered facades matte and even in appearance, with a fine, subtle surface grain. Preserve their original base colors and color zoning. Avoid broad mottling, cloudy patches, pronounced trowel marks, uneven relief or a distressed plaster appearance.
