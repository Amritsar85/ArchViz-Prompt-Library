# PEOPLE_02 — Subtle Human Activity

Introduce a limited number of realistic human figures in suitable pedestrian areas to create a subtle sense of everyday activity.

Ensure all figures have correct scale, believable posture, natural clothing and physically coherent shadows.

Distribute people naturally according to the existing circulation paths, entrances, public spaces and architectural context.

Avoid crowding, repetitive poses, exaggerated activity and unrealistic human placement.

Preserve all existing architectural elements, landscape structures and camera composition.
