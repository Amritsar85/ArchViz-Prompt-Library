# VEGETATION_02 — Maximum Foliage Sharpness

Preserve the original vegetation geometry, sharpness, branching structure, foliage silhouettes and fine botanical details.

Trees, shrubs and planted areas must remain naturally detailed and clearly resolved, especially in the foreground and midground.

Maintain distinct foliage clusters, readable branching patterns, irregular canopy shapes, realistic leaf density and natural color variation.

Improve photographic integration through believable lighting, realistic contact shadows and subtle tonal adjustments.

Avoid soft, smeared, blurry or painterly vegetation.

Preserve natural atmospheric softness only in the far background, not in foreground planting.

Do not replace, simplify or unnecessarily reconstruct existing vegetation.
