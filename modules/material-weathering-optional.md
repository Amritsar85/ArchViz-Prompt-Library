# MATERIALS_02 — Optional Weathering / Patina (OPT-IN ONLY)

Use this module only when the user explicitly requests aged, lived-in, weathered or neglected material surfaces.

Introduce restrained, physically plausible weathering limited to the specifically requested materials or surfaces. Adapt weathering to material behavior, climate, exposure and surface orientation. Keep the extent and intensity under control.

Do not automatically extend dirt, mold, staining or water streaks to other walls or architectural materials. Preserve the original building design, material identity and geometry.

Suggested intensity: light / moderate / heavy. Default: OFF.
