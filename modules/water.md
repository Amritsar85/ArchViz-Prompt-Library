# WATER_01 — Realistic Water

Enhance the realism of the water while preserving its existing geometry and position.

Create physically believable water with natural ripples, subtle surface variation, realistic reflections, depth-dependent color changes, and convincing interaction with surrounding edges, boats, quay walls, or waterfront elements where present.

Maintain a calm and natural appearance appropriate to the scene.

Avoid artificial water patterns, exaggerated wave motion, unrealistic reflections, or overly glossy CGI-looking surfaces.
