# CAMERA_01 — Professional Architectural Photography

Make the scene resemble a genuine photograph captured by a professional architectural photographer using high-quality camera optics.

Maintain realistic lens behavior, natural tonal response, authentic photographic microcontrast, coherent focus and believable exposure.

Preserve the original camera viewpoint, field of view, perspective and composition.

Ensure foreground and midground architectural details remain naturally resolved.

Avoid artificial depth-of-field effects, unrealistic lens distortion, exaggerated sharpening, excessive noise reduction and synthetic photographic filters.
