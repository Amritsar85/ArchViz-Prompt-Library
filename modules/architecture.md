# ARCHITECTURE_01 — Fine Architectural Details

Improve the visual clarity and realism of existing architectural details.

Preserve precise rooflines, window frames, facade joints, structural connections, parapets, railings, doors, masonry and ornamental elements where present.

Enhance fine surface definition and material transitions without inventing new architectural components.

Maintain correct proportions, construction logic and perspective.

Avoid distorted details, duplicated openings, invented decorations and artificial geometric sharpening.
