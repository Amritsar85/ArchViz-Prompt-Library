# SHARPNESS_01 — Natural Optical Sharpness

Maintain crisp and naturally resolved details throughout the image.

Preserve clear architectural edges, readable material textures, defined rooflines, sharp window frames, and realistic environmental detail.

Maintain naturally resolved vegetation and accurate fine textures appropriate to the camera distance.

Use realistic photographic sharpness and subtle microcontrast without artificial oversharpening.

Avoid smudged textures, soft blurry surfaces, painterly detail loss, sharpening halos, artificial high-frequency noise, or excessive diffusion.
